How it works

MagicLists reads your library's metadata and sonic analysis to generate playlists that feel curated, not random.

Each playlist is built using versioned "recipes" that run locally or through your chosen AI provider, then saved straight to your Navidrome library.

Features

Song Radio

Starts with one track and finds others that sound alike, arranging them into a cohesive, radio-style flow.

Sonic Analysis

Maps your entire library's sound profile so similarity matching is fast and accurate.

This Is

Builds a definitive single-artist mix — balancing hits, features, and deep cuts.

Re-Discover

Uses your listening history to identify tracks you once played often but haven’t heard recently. AI then curates a playlist that balances variety and familiarity for a meaningful rediscovery experience.

Genre Mix

Takes the best of a selected genre from your library and uses AI to craft a fresh, engaging playlist.

Auto-Refresh

Keeps playlists up to date automatically — daily, weekly, or monthly.

Privacy & control

Your music and metadata remain yours. No music files are uploaded — you choose whether AI runs locally or via a remote API key.

About this project

MagicLists is an open-source experiment in AI-assisted music discovery for people who run their own Navidrome server. Instead of "renting" playlists from Apple or Spotify, you keep full control of your library and still enjoy smart, personalised mixes.

This is an ongoing research project led by a product designer with 20+ years in tech, built with and for the Navidrome community. Together we're proving that AI-powered curation can be free, transparent and privacy-respecting.
